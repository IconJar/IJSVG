Quartz renderer benchmark — 2026-10-01

Implementation
IJSVG draws its parsed IJSVGRootNode through IJSVGQuartzRenderer. The renderer
resolves NSObject paint objects (shape, gradient, image, pattern, group, mask,
transform, root and filter). It neither creates nor traverses CALayer objects.
The original IJSVGLayerTree and layer renderer remain available unchanged.
Both backends share the existing Core Image/Metal filter evaluator and shaders.
Quartz bitmap destinations cache pattern images; PDF destinations use CGPattern.

Measurement
Machine architecture: arm64
Framework: Xcode Debug build; benchmark harness: clang -O2, ARC.
11 SVG fixtures at 64, 256 and 900 square pixels, sRGB RGBA8, backing scale 1.
Parsing is excluded. First draw includes tree/paint preparation and drawing;
these are single observations, not statistically robust cold-start estimates.
The very first legacy draw also includes process/framework warmup.
Warm times are medians of 15 draws after first draw plus 3 warmups. Backend order
alternates. Context allocation and clearing are outside the timed region.
The Quartz measurement uses IJSVG's default draw API; the legacy measurement
calls the retained root layer renderer with equivalent quality and batching.

Results
Warm speedup geometric mean over all 33 cases: 1.33x.
Range: 0.86x–2.21x. Values below 1 mean Quartz was slower.
Car at 256: 8.1414 ms -> 6.3690 ms (1.28x).
Car at 900: 31.8611 ms -> 24.7327 ms (1.29x).
Image at 256: 0.5102 ms -> 0.3119 ms (1.64x).
Clipped at 256: 0.1497 ms -> 0.0990 ms (1.51x).
Pattern-alignment at 256: 0.2085 ms -> 0.2435 ms (0.86x).
5x5 checkerboard at 900: 0.1200 ms -> 0.1303 ms (0.92x).
Debug measurements are comparative observations, not release-performance claims.

Correctness
The harness intercepts CALayer allocation and its base renderInContext: method.
All Quartz first/warm draws recorded zero allocations and zero rendering calls.
It fails if either counter is nonzero. Pixel comparisons cover all four channels.
All non-pattern fixtures matched exactly or within one 8-bit channel value.
5x5 checkerboard matched exactly. Diagonal stripes differ by at most 2 at 64 px
and match exactly at 256/900. Translated pattern alignment has edge resampling
variations: mean absolute channel errors 0.8278 / 0.3416 / 0.1232 out of 255 at
64 / 256 / 900; maximum errors 35 / 31 / 46. It is not pixel-identical.
Pattern checks also verify repetition at known SVG coordinates.
The full 322-test suite passed. After final context/lifetime changes and adding
a destination-origin test, all 5 focused renderer/filter-paint checks passed.

Reproduce
From IJSVGExample, build in Xcode, then:
sh Benchmarks/run-quartz.sh /path/to/Build/Products/Debug /tmp/ijsvg-quartz
This writes timings, comparison metrics, and PNGs for both backends.
Raw measurements: Benchmarks/quartz-results.csv.
